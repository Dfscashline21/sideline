# M3.10 · STAR story from your current job

> **Ticket:** [#55](https://github.com/Dfscashline21/sideline/issues/55) ·
> **Mode:** hand · **Time:** 45 min ·
> **Prompt:** *A reporting inconsistency at work caused by unclear grain
> or missing history, and the model fix.*

The STAR format, word budgets and common mistakes are in the
[M0.9 lab](../m0/m0.9-star-story.md). Reread its table before starting.
This lab covers only what's specific to a data-modelling story.

## Finding the story

Questions to jog memory:

- **Grain:** two dashboards showing different totals for "the same"
  number. A join that multiplied rows (orders joined to order lines
  joined to shipments). A "per customer" table that was really per
  customer per account. Someone averaging a percentage.
- **Missing history:** a report that changed its past numbers after a
  customer moved region, a salesperson changed team, or a product
  changed category: an SCD Type 1 problem. An "as of last quarter"
  question nobody could answer because attributes were overwritten.
- **Identity:** the same customer counted twice across two systems. Two
  customers merged by mistake. That's the M3.4 crosswalk problem at work.

If you fixed it with a query patch rather than a model change, the
story still works. Make the Action about what you learned and what you
proposed so it couldn't happen again.

## What makes a modelling story strong

- **Name the grain explicitly.** "The table was one row per order line,
  but the report treated it as one row per order" is a sentence
  interviewers remember. It shows you think in grains.
- **A before/after number:** revenue misreported by $n or n%, n hours
  a week reconciling, n reports affected, n customers double-counted.
- **The prevention, not just the fix:** a uniqueness test, a documented
  grain, an SCD2 dimension, a review step.

## Follow-ups to prepare

Write a one-line answer to each:

1. "How did you find the root cause?" (Ideally: you compared row counts
   before and after a join, or found a duplicated key.)
2. "Why not just fix the report?"
3. "How would you design it from scratch today?" (Your M3.2 and M3.3 work
   is the answer.)

## Where to save it

Private notes alongside your other STAR stories, **never** this repo.
Close the issue with "Saved to private notes" and no details.
