# M3.5 · Design dim_team, dim_season, dim_game

> **Ticket:** [#50](https://github.com/Dfscashline21/sideline/issues/50) ·
> **Mode:** hand · **Time:** 3 hours ·
> **Needs:** M3.2 (grains), M3.3 (SCD2 conventions)

## Why this matters

These three look boring next to `dim_player`, but they're where
multi-league models break:

- **Teams move, rename and switch conferences.** A "Pac-12 vs Big Ten"
  report is wrong for 2024-25 if UCLA's conference is overwritten.
- **Leagues run on different calendars.** The NBA season spans two years,
  the WNBA's sits inside one, college basketball spans two but starts
  later. A bare `season = 2026` means different things in each.
- **Not every game counts the same.** Preseason, play-in, the NBA Cup
  final, the NCAA tournament: game type decides what goes into standings
  and season averages.

These become the conformed dimensions of M5.8, shared by every fact
and every league.

## What you'll produce

`dim_team`, `dim_season` and `dim_game` sections in

- the UCLA and USC move to the Big Ten in 2024, modelled

## Part 1 · dim_team (60 min)

### 1. Find the changes in real data

```sql
select team_id, team_display_name, min(season), max(season)
from nba_team_box
where team_id in (3, 17, 25, 30)
group by all
order by team_id, 3;
```

You'll see relocations and renames under a stable ESPN `team_id`:
SuperSonics → Thunder, New Jersey → Brooklyn, and two different
franchises that have both been called "Charlotte Hornets". Notice one
name is stored with an embedded line break (`NO/Oklahoma City\r\n
Hornets`). That's raw data for you.

WNBA expansion is in your data too: the Golden State Valkyries joined
in 2025, Portland Fire and Toronto Tempo in 2026.

### 2. Decide what's tracked, and how

| Attribute | Changes because | SCD type? |
|---|---|---|
| Name, city, abbreviation | Relocation, rebrand | ? |
| Conference, division | Realignment | ? |
| Colours, logo | Rebrand | ? |
| Arena | Moves, naming rights | Probably belongs in `dim_venue` |

<details>
<summary>Hint 1 · Nudge: conference by date or by season?</summary>

Conference membership changes take effect on a date (UCLA and USC
officially joined the Big Ten on 2 August 2024), but basketball
questions are asked **by season**: "Big Ten teams in 2024-25". If
`valid_from` is 2 August 2024, does every 2024-25 game fall after it?
Would season-based validity be simpler?

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2 · Approach</summary>

Two reasonable designs:

1. **`dim_team` as SCD2** with dated validity. Works for name and city.
   Conference changes are just another attribute change.
2. **SCD2 for identity attributes, plus a separate
   `team_conference_season` table** (one row per team per league
   season, with conference and division). Membership questions become a
   simple join on season, with no date ranges.

Pick one and say why. The course default is option 1 for simplicity;
option 2 is better if you'll report by conference a lot.

</details>

### 3. Model the realignment example

Write the `dim_team` rows for UCLA from 2023-24 to 2025-26, and show
which row a game on 2024-02-10 and one on 2025-02-10 join to.

<details>
<summary>Solution: UCLA rows (option 1)</summary>

| team_sk | team_id | league | name | conference | valid_from | valid_to | is_current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 501 | (ESPN id) | ncaa_m | UCLA Bruins | Pac-12 | (history start) | 2024-08-02 | false |
| 877 | (ESPN id) | ncaa_m | UCLA Bruins | Big Ten | 2024-08-02 | 9999-12-31 | true |

- The 2024-02-10 game (2023-24 season) joins to row 501, Pac-12.
- The 2025-02-10 game (2024-25 season) joins to row 877, Big Ten.
- USC gets the same pair of rows. The women's teams (`ncaa_w`) are
  separate teams with their own rows: same school, different league.

</details>

✅ **Checkpoint:** "Pac-12 wins in 2023-24" and "Big Ten wins in
2024-25" both count UCLA correctly.

## Part 2 · dim_season (45 min)

### 4. Write out the three calendars

| League | Typical span | Human label | ESPN `season` value |
|---|---|---|---|
| NBA | October to June | 2026-27 | 2027 |
| WNBA | May to October | 2026 | 2026 |
| NCAA (men's and women's) | November to April | 2026-27 | 2027 |

### 5. Design the key

<details>
<summary>Hint · Nudge</summary>

Could `season = 2027` be the key? What does it mean for the WNBA? What
happens when you join a WNBA fact to `dim_season` on year alone?

</details>

A key must be unique **across leagues**. Options:

- composite natural key `(league, season_label)` plus a surrogate `season_sk`
- a readable string key, e.g. `nba_2026-27`, `wnba_2026`

Either way, store the dates that matter:

| Column | Example (NBA 2026-27) |
|---|---|
| `season_sk` | surrogate |
| `league` | `nba` |
| `season_label` | `2026-27` |
| `start_year`, `end_year` | 2026, 2027 |
| `source_season` | 2027 (what ESPN calls it) |
| `regular_season_start`, `regular_season_end` | from the schedule |
| `postseason_start`, `postseason_end` | from the schedule |

✅ **Checkpoint:** write the two rows for **NBA 2026-27** and **WNBA
2026**. Their keys differ, neither is ambiguous, and both map back to
their ESPN `season` value.

## Part 3 · dim_game (45 min)

### 6. List the game types

Look at what `season_type` gives you in the M1 data (2, 3 and 5), then
list what Sideline actually needs to distinguish:

- NBA: preseason, regular season, NBA Cup group and knockout games,
  NBA Cup final, play-in, playoffs, All-Star.
- WNBA: regular season, Commissioner's Cup final, playoffs, All-Star.
- College: regular season, conference tournament, NCAA tournament,
  NIT and other postseason events.

<details>
<summary>Hint · The NBA Cup trap</summary>

NBA Cup group-stage and knockout games count in the regular-season
standings. The **final** doesn't. That's why the two finalists showed
83 "regular season" games in M1. A single `game_type` column can't
express that. Add flags such as `counts_in_standings` and
`counts_in_season_stats`, so queries don't have to remember the rule.

</details>

### 7. Write the column list

At minimum: `game_sk`, `league`, `source_game_id`, `season_sk`,
`game_date`, `game_type`, `counts_in_standings`, `home_team_id`,
`away_team_id`, `venue_sk`, `neutral_site`, `status` (scheduled, final,
postponed, cancelled).

Is `dim_game` SCD2? Think about what changes: status (scheduled →
final), date (postponed). Do you need the history, or only the
latest? (Kimball would treat this as an accumulating snapshot. M3.1
session 2 has the idea.)

## Gotchas

- **Same name, different franchise.** "Charlotte Hornets" was ESPN team
  3 in 2002 and team 30 from 2015. Never key on team name.
- **Franchise history vs team ID.** The NBA officially credits the
  1988-2002 Hornets history to today's Charlotte team, while ESPN keeps
  it under the New Orleans `team_id`. Which does Sideline follow? Write
  it down.
- **Neutral sites.** Tournament games have no real home team. The
  `home_away` columns in the source still say "home".
