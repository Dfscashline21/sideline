# M2.9 · Ready-when check (closed-book)

> **Ticket:** [#44](https://github.com/Dfscashline21/sideline/issues/44) ·
> **Mode:** hand, closed-book · **Time:** 45 to 60 min ·
> **Needs:** M2.3 to M2.8 merged

## Why this matters

The M2 promise: *kill the job mid-run, restart it, and end with exactly
the right data and no duplicates.* M2.7 proved the code does it. This
check proves **you** can explain and demonstrate it without the labs, the
way you'd have to in an interview or during an incident at 2 a.m.

## The rules

- **No AI, no notes, no labs, no reading your own code** until you've
  finished the explanation part.
- You may use a terminal and the MinIO console for the demonstration.
- Talk out loud and record yourself if you can.

## Part 1 · Explain it on a whiteboard (20 min, timed)

Draw and explain, without looking anything up:

1. **The run, step by step:** game log → per-game objects → manifest →
   watermark. Mark the commit point.
2. **The key layout:** write the full key for one play-by-play object,
   and say what each segment is for.
3. **Three crash points,** and for each: what's in MinIO, what the
   watermark says, and exactly what the restart does:
   - after the game log, before any game object
   - halfway through the game objects
   - after the manifest, before the watermark
4. **Why no duplicates:** in one sentence, using the word *deterministic*.
5. **Why retries are safe:** which failures you retry, the backoff
   formula with jitter, and why jitter.

Stop the timer. If you needed to look at anything, note what.

## Part 2 · Demonstrate it (20 to 30 min)

With `make up` running, and still without notes:

1. Clear a test prefix (or use a fresh one).
2. Start a backfill of a short date range.
3. Kill it with `kill -9` partway through. Show from the logs that it
   really died mid-run.
4. Show the state: which objects exist, that there's no manifest for the
   run, and that the watermark hasn't moved.
5. Restart it. Show the log reporting skipped objects.
6. Show the result matches a clean run: run your M2.7 script, or compare
   the key lists and checksums by hand.

If you can't remember how to do a step, that's the gap to fix.

## Part 3 · Score yourself honestly

| Result | Meaning |
|---|---|
| All three crash points explained correctly, demo works first time | **Pass.** Close the ticket. |
| One crash point wrong, or the demo needed one look at your code | Not yet. Redo M2.7 steps 1 and 5 from a blank page, retry in 2 days. |
| Can't explain why keys are deterministic, or the demo produced duplicates | Repeat M2.2 step 4 and M2.7 from scratch, then retry. |
