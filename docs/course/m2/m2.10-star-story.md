# M2.10 · STAR story from your current job

> **Ticket:** [#45](https://github.com/Dfscashline21/sideline/issues/45) ·
> **Mode:** hand · **Time:** 45 min ·
> **Prompt:** *A pipeline at work that duplicated or lost data on retry,
> and how you'd make it idempotent.*

The STAR format, word budgets and common mistakes are in the
[M0.9 lab](../m0/m0.9-star-story.md). Reread its table before starting.
This lab covers only what's specific to an idempotency story.

## Finding the story

Questions to jog memory:

- **Duplicates:** a job that was rerun after a failure and doubled a
  day's rows. An `INSERT` without a key. Files named with a timestamp
  that got loaded twice. An event consumer that reprocessed messages
  after a restart.
- **Losses:** a watermark (or "last run time") updated *before* the load
  finished, so a crash skipped a window for ever. A truncate-then-load
  that failed between the two steps. A retry loop that gave up silently.
- **Silent wrongness:** reports that drifted from the source and nobody
  knew why until someone reconciled counts.

Note the prompt's wording: "how you'd make it idempotent". If the fix
never shipped, the story is still valid. Your Action is the diagnosis
plus the design you proposed, and the Result is what it would have
prevented (estimated, and said so).

## What makes an idempotency story strong

- **A number.** Duplicate rows, overstated revenue, hours of manual
  cleanup, reruns per month, people who consumed the bad numbers.
- **The mechanism, precisely.** Not "it was flaky", but "the job wrote
  its checkpoint before the load, so a timeout after the checkpoint
  skipped six hours of events." The M2.1 vocabulary (deterministic keys,
  commit point, watermark written last) is what makes this land.
- **How you'd prove the fix.** "I'd kill it mid-run in a test and compare
  with a clean run" is the M2.7 answer, and it signals seniority.

## Follow-ups to prepare

Write a one-line answer to each:

1. "How did you find the duplicates?" (A reconciliation count? A
   uniqueness check? A user complaint?)
2. "What's the difference between idempotent and exactly-once?"
3. "What stops it happening again?" (A test, a unique constraint, a
   design rule, a review checklist?)

## Where to save it

Private notes alongside the earlier stories, **never** this repo. Close
the issue with "Saved to private notes" and no details.
