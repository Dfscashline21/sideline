# M13.7 · Ready-when check (closed-book)

> **Ticket:** [#168](https://github.com/Dfscashline21/sideline/issues/168) ·
> **Mode:** hand, closed-book · **Time:** 75 min ·
> **Needs:** M13.6 done (four mocks)

## Why this matters

The M13 promise: *run a 45-minute system design interview end to end,
out loud, with a timer.* M13.6 had an interviewer prompting you. This
check has nobody: you run the whole thing yourself, which proves the
framework is in your head and not in the agent's prompts.

## The rules

- **No AI, no notes, no framework page, no design docs.** Just a timer, a
  blank whiteboard (or Excalidraw), and a recording.
- Speak the whole time, as if an interviewer were listening.
- Where an interviewer would answer a question, say your assumption out
  loud and move on.

## Steps

### 1. Pick a problem you haven't designed

Three sealed problems. Pick one without reading the others (roll a die:
1–2 → A, 3–4 → B, 5–6 → C). Save the rest for IP.3.

<details>
<summary>Problem A</summary>

Design the data platform for a **fantasy basketball** app: users draft
players, scores update during live games, weekly leaderboards, and the
product team wants daily engagement analytics.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Problem B</summary>

Design a pipeline that ingests **stadium ticket scans and concession
sales** from 30 arenas and gives each arena operations team a dashboard
within 5 minutes of activity.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Problem C</summary>

Design a **sports content recommendation** data pipeline: article views,
video plays and app events from 2 million daily users feed a model that
updates recommendations hourly.

</details>

### 2. Run it: 45 minutes, recorded

Hit record and start the timer. Follow your framework from memory:
requirements and estimates, data flow, deep dive, quality and failure
modes, security and cost, evolution. Check the time at roughly 8, 15,
30 and 38 minutes; that was your M13.1 budget.

### 3. Self-review with the M13.6 rubric (20 min)

Watch the recording. Score each area 1 to 5, honestly. Then check against
your framework page: which of the ten areas did you skip entirely?

### 4. Score the result

| Result | Meaning |
|---|---|
| All phases reached in 45 min, every area at least touched, no score below 3 | **Pass.** Close the ticket. |
| Ran out of time before failure modes/evolution | Not yet. Redo with a stricter time budget in 2 days. |
| Froze or lost structure | Rewrite the framework page from memory, then do another M13.6 mock before retrying. |

### 5. Write the notes

Append to `docs/notes/m13.md`:

```markdown
## Ready-when check (YYYY-MM-DD)
- Problem: A/B/C · Finished at: mm:ss · Result: pass / retry
- Lowest-scoring area and why:
- Areas skipped:

## Hardest part of M13
<three sentences: what was hardest and why>
```

PR with `Closes #168` once you've passed.
