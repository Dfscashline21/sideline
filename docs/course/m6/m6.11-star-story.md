# M6.11 · STAR story from your current job

> **Ticket:** [#93](https://github.com/Dfscashline21/sideline/issues/93) ·
> **Mode:** hand · **Time:** 45 min ·
> **Prompt:** *A scheduling or dependency failure at work (cron chains,
> missed upstream) and the orchestration fix.*

The STAR format, word budgets and common mistakes are in the
[M0.9 lab](../m0/m0.9-star-story.md). Reread its table before starting.
This lab covers only what's specific to an orchestration story.

## Finding the story

Questions to jog memory:

- **Cron chains:** job B scheduled at 3 a.m. "because A usually finishes
  by 2:30". One night A ran late and B processed yesterday's data, or
  half of today's.
- **Missed upstream:** a report built before a file arrived; a vendor
  feed that silently stopped and nobody noticed for days.
- **Reruns:** a manual rerun that double-loaded a table; a backfill that
  had to be done by hand, day by day.
- **Hidden dependencies:** a team changed a table your job read, and
  nobody knew the dependency existed.

Your fix doesn't have to be a new orchestrator. Strong fixes include:
replacing a time dependency with a data dependency (sensor, file
arrival, upstream success), making a load idempotent so reruns are
safe, adding a lateness alert, or documenting a dependency so changes
get reviewed.

## What makes this story strong

- **The failure mode named precisely:** "a time-based dependency where
  a data-based one was needed". That phrasing shows M6 stuck.
- **A number:** how late or wrong the data was, how many people or
  reports were affected, how many hours the manual fixes took, how many
  incidents before vs after.
- **Prevention, not just the fix.** What stops the next one: an alert,
  a test, a runbook (M6.6 gives you vocabulary).

## Follow-ups to prepare

Write a one-line answer to each:

1. "Why not just move job B to 4 a.m.?"
2. "How would you backfill the bad days safely?"
3. "How would you know if it happened again?"

## Where to save it

Private notes alongside your other STAR stories, **never** this repo.
Close the issue with "Saved to private notes" and no details.

## Done when

- [ ] S, T, A, R in under 200 words
- [ ] Includes a number (time saved, cost, incidents, rows or people)
- [ ] Saved to private notes, not the public repo
- [ ] Three follow-up answers drafted
