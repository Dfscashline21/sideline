# M6.10 · Ready-when check (closed-book)

> **Ticket:** [#92](https://github.com/Dfscashline21/sideline/issues/92) ·
> **Mode:** hand, closed-book · **Time:** 60 to 90 min ·
> **Needs:** M6.4 to M6.9 done

## Why this matters

The M6 promise: *backfill a month of data while daily runs continue,
without duplicates or conflicts*, and explain why it's safe. In an
interview this is a whiteboard question ("walk me through a backfill on
your platform"); here you do it for real, then explain it with no notes.

## The rules

- **No AI, no notes, no docs, no labs** from the moment you start.
- You may use the terminal, the Dagster UI and your own code: an
  on-call engineer has those.
- Talk out loud, or record yourself explaining as you go.

## Steps

### 1. Pick a month you haven't backfilled

Choose a league and a month **not** covered by M6.7, or one you'll wipe
first. For example WNBA July 2023, or NBA February 2014 (outside
M6.7's range). Write down, *before starting*, the current row counts of
the affected facts and serving tables for that month.

### 2. Run it (closed-book)

1. Make sure daily runs are live: the sensor is on, or you'll trigger a
   current-date run by hand halfway through.
2. Launch the backfill for that month.
3. While it runs, trigger at least one daily run and watch it.
4. Run your duplicate checks and count checks when it's done.

Time it. If you need to look something up, note what. That's your
revision list.

### 3. Explain why it was safe (closed-book, 5 minutes)

Out loud or written, cover:

- **Idempotency:** why rerunning any partition gives the same result
- **Isolation:** why the backfill and a daily run can't corrupt each
  other, even on the same date
- **Concurrency:** what limits backfill runs, what serialises shared
  writes, and what keeps daily runs moving
- **Quality gate:** what stops a bad partition being published
- **Failure:** what happens if one backfill run fails halfway

Then draw it: the asset graph, where partitions split, and where the
concurrency limits apply.

### 4. Check against reality

Notes and labs allowed again. Compare your explanation with your config
and code. Anything you said that isn't actually true of your system?

### 5. Score yourself

| Result | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Backfill clean, daily runs fine, explanation covers all five points correctly | **Pass.** Close the ticket |
| Backfill clean but explanation had gaps | Redo M6.1 session 2 and M6.7 step 2, retry the explanation in 2 days |
| Duplicates or conflicts | That's a real bug. Fix it (that's the most valuable outcome of this check), then retry with another month |

### 6. Write the notes

Append to `docs/notes/m6.md`:

```markdown

- League/month: · Partitions: · Wall time:
- Daily runs during backfill: count, queue wait, outcome
- Duplicate checks: results
- Looked up during the check:

## Hardest part of M6
<three sentences: what was hardest and why>
```
