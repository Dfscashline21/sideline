# M11.8 · STAR story from your current job

> **Ticket:** [#151](https://github.com/Dfscashline21/sideline/issues/151) ·
> **Mode:** hand · **Time:** 45 min ·
> **Prompt:** *A team at work that couldn't get the data it needed in the
> form it needed, and the serving fix.*

The STAR format, word budgets and common mistakes are in the
[M0.9 lab](../m0/m0.9-star-story.md). Reread its table before starting.
This lab covers only what's specific to a serving story.

## Finding the story

Questions to jog memory:

- **Wrong shape:** a team exporting CSVs from a dashboard every week to
  rebuild the same pivot in Excel. An app team calling the warehouse
  directly, or scraping a report.
- **Wrong speed:** a dashboard that took minutes to load, so nobody used
  it. A nightly extract that was always a day late for a morning meeting.
- **Wrong definition:** two teams reporting different numbers for the
  same metric, because each wrote its own SQL.
- **Wrong place:** sales or support needing a number inside their own
  tool (CRM, ticketing), copying it in by hand (a reverse-ETL story).

The fix doesn't have to be an API. A curated table, a shared metric
definition, a scheduled extract or a documented data product all count.

## What makes a serving story strong

- **The consumer is the hero's customer.** Name who was blocked and what
  they couldn't do, in their terms (not "the table was denormalised").
- **You picked a pattern on purpose.** Say what you considered and why
  you chose this one. That's M11.1 in action.
- **A number:** hours of manual work removed per week, load time before
  and after, number of people or teams now self-serving, requests to
  your team that stopped.

## Follow-ups to prepare

Write a one-line answer to each:

1. "How did you know they were actually using it afterwards?"
2. "What would you do differently if ten times as many people needed it?"
3. "Who owns it now?" (Your M11.6 data product page idea.)

## Where to save it

Private notes alongside your earlier STAR stories, **never** this repo.
Close the issue with "Saved to private notes" and no details.

## Done when

- [ ] S, T, A, R in under 200 words
- [ ] Includes a number (time, cost, incidents, rows, or people)
- [ ] Saved to private notes, not the public repo
- [ ] Three follow-up answers drafted
