# M8.12 · STAR story from your current job

> **Ticket:** [#118](https://github.com/Dfscashline21/sideline/issues/118) ·
> **Mode:** hand · **Time:** 45 min ·
> **Prompt:** *A real-time or near-real-time need at work, and whether
> streaming was actually the right call.*

The STAR format, word budgets and common mistakes are in the
[M0.9 lab](../m0/m0.9-star-story.md). Reread its table before starting.
This lab covers only what's specific to a streaming story.

## Notice the prompt's twist

It doesn't ask "tell me about a streaming system you built". It asks
whether streaming was **the right call**. The best version of this story
is often one where you **didn't** build a stream processor, or where you
pushed back on "real-time" and found out what freshness the business
actually needed. Interviewers hear plenty of Kafka stories; they rarely
hear a candidate say "we measured, and hourly batch was enough".

## Finding the story

Questions to jog memory:

- Someone asked for a "real-time dashboard". What did they actually do
  with it, and how often did they look?
- Was there an alert, fraud check, inventory count or SLA that really
  did need seconds? What did it cost when it was late?
- Did a team build streaming infrastructure that ended up feeding a
  daily report? What would you have done instead?
- Did you replace a nightly job with CDC or micro-batches? What changed
  for the users, measurably?
- Did late or duplicate events ever cause a wrong number? (Your M8.5
  and M8.7 vocabulary applies directly.)

## What makes this story strong

- **The freshness requirement, as a number,** and how you found it:
  "the ops team checked it twice a day" or "decisions were made within
  5 minutes of an order".
- **The cost of the alternative:** infrastructure, on-call load, people.
- **A result with a number:** latency, cost, incidents, hours saved.
- **One technical detail to go deep on:** how late data or duplicates
  were handled, or why micro-batches were enough.

## Follow-ups to prepare

Write a one-line answer to each:

1. "How did you decide what latency was actually needed?"
2. "What would have made you change your mind?" (Your ADR-006 "revisit
   when" is practice for exactly this.)
3. "How did you handle late or duplicate data?"

## Where to save it

Private notes alongside your other STAR stories, **never** this repo.
Close the issue with "Saved to private notes" and no details.
