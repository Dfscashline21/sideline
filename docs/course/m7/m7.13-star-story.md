# M7.13 · STAR story from your current job

> **Ticket:** [#106](https://github.com/Dfscashline21/sideline/issues/106) ·
> **Mode:** hand · **Time:** 45 min ·
> **Prompt:** *A job at work that was slow or expensive at scale, and what
> you'd change.*

The STAR format, word budgets and common mistakes are in the
[M0.9 lab](../m0/m0.9-star-story.md). Reread its table before starting.
This lab covers only what's specific to a scale story.

## Finding the story

The prompt says "and what you'd change", so a story where you
**diagnosed** the problem but haven't fixed it yet still works. Make the
Action your diagnosis and proposal. Questions to jog memory:

- **Slow:** a nightly job that crept from 1 hour to 6 as data grew. A
  report that times out at month-end. A backfill that took a weekend.
- **Expensive:** a warehouse bill dominated by one query or one
  dashboard. A cluster sized for the peak and idle the rest of the time.
  Full-table rebuilds where incremental would do.
- **Fragile at scale:** a job that fails only on the biggest customer,
  the biggest day or the end of quarter (often skew).

It doesn't have to be Spark. Any engine works: SQL Server, Snowflake,
pandas, cron scripts. The concepts from this module (shuffles, skew,
join strategy, "does this even need a distributed engine?") apply to all
of them.

## What makes a scale story strong

- **The diagnosis is the story.** "I looked at the query profile and saw
  one step reading 900M rows to produce 2M" shows the senior skill.
  Name the tool you used to look (query profile, Spark UI, `EXPLAIN`, logs).
- **Before and after numbers:** runtime, cost per run or per month,
  rows processed, compute hours. If there's no "after" yet, give the
  estimate and how you arrived at it.
- **A trade-off you considered.** Bigger cluster vs fixing the job.
  Incremental vs full rebuild (and its correctness risk). Interviewers
  probe whether you just threw money at it.

## Follow-ups to prepare

Write a one-line answer to each:

1. "How did you find the bottleneck?"
2. "Why not just scale up the cluster or warehouse?"
3. "How would you know if it regressed?" (a runtime alert, a cost
   dashboard, a benchmark in CI?)

## Where to save it

Private notes alongside your other STAR stories, **never** this repo.
Close the issue with "Saved to private notes" and no details.

## Done when

- [ ] S, T, A, R in under 200 words
- [ ] Includes a number (runtime, cost, rows or compute hours)
- [ ] Saved to private notes, not the public repo
- [ ] Three follow-up answers drafted
