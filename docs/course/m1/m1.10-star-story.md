# M1.10 · STAR story from your current job

> **Ticket:** [#35](https://github.com/Dfscashline21/sideline/issues/35) ·
> **Mode:** hand · **Time:** 45 min ·
> **Prompt:** *A query or report at work that was wrong or slow, and how
> window functions or a better plan fixed it.*

The STAR format, word budgets and common mistakes are in the
[M0.9 lab](../m0/m0.9-star-story.md). Reread its table before starting.
This lab covers only what's specific to an SQL story.

## Finding the story

Questions to jog memory:

- **Wrong:** a report whose numbers didn't match another report. A
  dashboard that double-counted after a join fanned out. A "latest
  record per customer" that sometimes returned two rows (`ROW_NUMBER`
  without a tiebreaker, or a `MAX(date)` self-join with ties).
- **Slow:** a query, dashboard or nightly job that took minutes or hours.
  A self-join or correlated subquery doing what one window function could.
  A filter applied after an expensive step instead of before.
- **Recurring:** the same logic copied into five reports, each slightly
  different. Did you consolidate it into one view or table (the M1.7
  summary-table idea)?

If your fix wasn't a window function, that's fine. The prompt says
"window functions **or a better plan**".

## What makes an SQL story strong

- **A before/after number.** Runtime (minutes → seconds), wrong rows
  (n records misreported), cost (warehouse credits), or people affected
  (n analysts using the report).
- **How you found the cause, not just the fix.** "I ran `EXPLAIN` and saw
  the join produced 40M rows from a 2M-row table" is the senior signal.
  Interviewers probe exactly here.
- **One technical detail you can go deep on.** Be ready to write the
  before and after query shape on a whiteboard, with the employer's
  names generalised (`orders`, `customers`), never real schemas.

## Follow-ups to prepare

Write a one-line answer to each:

1. "How did you know the new result was correct?" (Your M1.7 `EXCEPT`
   check is the textbook answer.)
2. "Why didn't you just add an index?"
3. "What stops this from happening again?" (A test? A shared view? A
   review checklist?)

## Where to save it

Private notes alongside the M0.9 story, **never** this repo. Close the
issue with "Saved to private notes" and no details.

## Done when

- [ ] S, T, A, R in under 200 words
- [ ] Includes a number (runtime, wrong rows, cost, or people)
